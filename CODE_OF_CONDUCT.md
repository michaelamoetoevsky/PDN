# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone. This includes people of any age, body size, or visible or invisible disability. It also includes any ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, or level of experience. Education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, and sexual identity and orientation are also included.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Show empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Give constructive feedback and accept it gracefully
- Accept responsibility and apologize to the people affected by our mistakes
- Focus on what is best for the overall community, not only for us as individuals

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, insults, and derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publication of the private information of other people without explicit permission
- Other conduct that can be reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions. This right applies to contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct. Leaders will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces. It also applies when an individual officially represents the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

You can report instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at conduct@pdn.example.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines to determine the consequences for any action that they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: A private written warning from community leaders. The warning clarifies the nature of the violation and explains why the behavior was inappropriate. Community leaders can request a public apology.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. For a specified period of time, you must not interact with the people involved. This includes unsolicited interaction with those who enforce the Code of Conduct. Avoid all interactions in community spaces and in external channels such as social media. Violation of these terms can lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from all interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. During this period, no public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed. This rule includes unsolicited interaction with those who enforce the Code of Conduct. Violation of these terms can lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: A pattern of violation of community standards. This includes sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, aggression toward classes of individuals, or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.
